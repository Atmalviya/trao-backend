One platform for clinical data at scale

Helix Health helps hospital networks normalise, govern and share patient data across departments. Our platform ingests HL7 and FHIR feeds, applies consent policies, and exposes audited APIs to internal analytics teams.

We operate in regulated environments — HIPAA and SOC 2 are table stakes. Engineering prioritises auditability, least-privilege access, and clear incident response.
