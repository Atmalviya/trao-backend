How we hire engineers

Helix interviews focus on regulated-domain judgement, not leetcode trivia. We publish our process so candidates can prepare honestly.

1. Recruiter screen (30 min)

Role fit, location, compensation band, timeline.

2. Practical coding (90 min, take-home optional path)

A data-pipeline or API exercise grounded in healthcare-adjacent scenarios — parsing, validation, audit logging. We review tests and error handling.

3. Architecture & compliance discussion (60 min)

Design a consent-aware data export service. We probe HIPAA-minded thinking, access controls, and operational monitoring — not memorised buzzwords.

4. Collaboration interview (45 min)

Behavioural examples: cross-team communication, incident response, mentoring.

We respond within a week of the final round.
