Careers at Vault

We are a team of 45 across Berlin and remote EU. Engineering-led culture.

Our interview process

  1. HM chat (30 min) — motivation and scope of role.
  2. Security engineering exercise (75 min) — review a small codebase for vulnerabilities, propose fixes, discuss trade-offs live.
  3. Pair programming (60 min) — extend a scanner rule in TypeScript with tests.
  4. Team fit (45 min) — ownership, communication, working with product.

No whiteboard algorithms. We care about secure coding instincts and clarity.
