Joining Acme: Our Interview Loop

We publish our hiring process in full because we think secrecy wastes everyone's time. Here is exactly what to expect when you interview for an engineering role at Acme.

1. Intro call (30 minutes)

A recruiter conversation about your background, motivation and logistics.

2. Take-home exercise (timeboxed to 3 hours)

A small, realistic backend problem. We ask you to build a service, write tests, and document your trade-offs in a README. We care about judgement, not completeness.

3. Technical deep-dive (60 minutes)

A pair of engineers walk through your take-home with you, then extend it live — adding a feature, discussing failure modes, and reviewing your testing approach.

4. System design interview (60 minutes)

We design a realistic distributed system together — for example, a fleet-coordination service that must stay correct under partial failure and network partitions. We look for how you reason about consistency, back-pressure and observability.

5. Values & collaboration interview (45 minutes)

A behavioural conversation about ownership, mentoring, handling disagreement, and how you communicate in writing. We ask for concrete past examples.

We aim to give a decision within five business days of the final round. Throughout, we value clear written communication and pragmatic engineering over trivia.
